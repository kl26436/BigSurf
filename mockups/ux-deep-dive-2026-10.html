Candidate 3 · styleOne row, one head, one field — and a rule for what a row promises

The same [icon][title/subtitle][trailing] row ships in at least nine classes, five section-head styles and five text-field classes. Worse than the class count: the promise is inconsistent — some rows look tappable and do nothing (.detail-row, .d-pr-row), one is tappable and doesn't look it (.rc-line). The fix is one component plus one rule: chevron = opens a page · value = static · control = acts.

Candidate 5 · usabilityGyms & machines — one word, one add flow, gym-aware by default

The same thing is called location (page, modals, More), gym (Settings subtitle, Equipment tab, aria-labels) and place. Adding a gym has two unrelated flows (GPS/map modal vs name-only prompt). "Add equipment" from the Equipment header creates a machine with no gym and lands on a detail page (≈12 taps to end up where the gym quick-add gets you in 6). The gym detail page with radius, size and "what's here" is built and unreachable. Tapping a Catalog row silently creates an equipment doc.
