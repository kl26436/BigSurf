3 — Goals & events (TP-1)

Lift, bodyweight, composition, consistency, balance — with or without a date, optionally tied to an event. The baseline is read from your data when you create the goal; you never type where you are. Status is ahead / on track / behind / met with the rate behind it in one sentence.
